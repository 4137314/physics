#+title: Frequency response, Bode plots and filters
#+summary: \(H(j\omega)\) is plotted as \(20\log|H|\) and \(\arg H\) vs \(\log\omega\) (Bode); each real pole bends the magnitude by −20 dB/decade and the phase by −90°, each zero the opposite; first-order RC sections give \(f_c=1/2\pi RC\), second-order sections add resonance with \(Q\).

* Method (Bode asymptotes)
Factor \(H(j\omega)=K\frac{\prod(1+j\omega/z_i)}{\prod(1+j\omega/p_k)}\) [cite:@bode1940]. Magnitude in dB is a sum: each factor is 0 dB below its corner and ±20 dB/dec above (exact error −3 dB at the corner). Phase: each factor contributes ±45° at its corner, ±90° asymptotically, transitions over one decade each side.

* Standard filters
| filter        | H(jω)                   | behaviour                      |
|---------------+-------------------------+--------------------------------|
| RC low-pass   | 1/(1 + jωRC)            | −20 dB/dec above f_c           |
| CR high-pass  | jωRC/(1 + jωRC)         | +20 dB/dec below f_c           |
| RLC band-pass | 1/(1 + jQ(ω/ω₀ − ω₀/ω)) | peak at ω₀, bandwidth ω₀/Q     |
| 2nd-order LP  | ω₀²/(ω₀² − ω² + jωω₀/Q) | −40 dB/dec, peaking if Q>0.707 |
Butterworth (\(Q=0.707\)) is maximally flat [cite:@butterworth1930].

* Worked example
Anti-aliasing for a 48 kHz ADC wanting ≥ 40 dB rejection at 24 kHz with a passband to 4 kHz: first order gives only \(20\log(24/4)=15.6\) dB; 4th-order Butterworth with \(f_c=8\) kHz gives \(80\log(3)=38\) dB at 24 kHz — close; use 5th order or oversampling.

* Engineering applications
Audio equalisers, anti-aliasing and reconstruction filters, loop stability (gain/phase margins of op-amps and PLLs), EMI filters, the frequency response of [[id:photodetectors][photoreceivers]] and of [[id:cmos-propagation-delay][interconnects]].

* Pitfall
dB of voltage ratios is \(20\log\), of power ratios \(10\log\); the "−3 dB" frequency is where *power* halves, i.e. \(|H|=1/\sqrt2\).
