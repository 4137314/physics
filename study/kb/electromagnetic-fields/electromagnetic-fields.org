#+title: Electromagnetic fields: the map of the subject
#+summary: From Coulomb, Ampère and Faraday to Maxwell's equations; then waves (plane waves, reflection, polarisation), guided waves (transmission lines, waveguides, S-parameters) and radiation (antennas): the classical limit of QED and the physics under every circuit and optical device.

* Overview
| block         | notes                                                     |
|---------------+-----------------------------------------------------------|
| static fields | vector calculus, Gauss, Laplace and capacitance, Ampère   |
| Maxwell       | Faraday, Maxwell's equations, potentials, Poynting        |
| waves         | plane waves in media, reflection and transmission         |
| guided waves  | transmission lines, Smith chart, S-parameters, waveguides |
| radiation     | dipoles, antenna parameters, Friis link budget            |

Capacitive sensors, motors and transformers, signal integrity on PCBs, RF front ends and antennas, microwave links, EMC, optical fibres and integrated photonics.
