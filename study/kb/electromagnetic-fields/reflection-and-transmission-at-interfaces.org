#+title: Reflection and transmission: Snell, Fresnel, Brewster and total internal reflection
#+summary: Phase matching along the interface gives \(n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2\); the boundary conditions give the Fresnel coefficients \(r_\perp=\frac{n_1\cos\theta_1-n_2\cos\theta_2}{n_1\cos\theta_1+n_2\cos\theta_2}\), \(r_\parallel=\frac{n_2\cos\theta_1-n_1\cos\theta_2}{n_2\cos\theta_1+n_1\cos\theta_2}\); \(r_\parallel=0\) at Brewster \(\tan\theta_B=n_2/n_1\), and beyond \(\sin\theta_c=n_2/n_1\) reflection is total with an evanescent field.

* Normal incidence
Continuity of \(E_t\) and \(H_t\): \(\Gamma=\frac{\eta_2-\eta_1}{\eta_2+\eta_1}=\frac{n_1-n_2}{n_1+n_2}\), \(\tau=1+\Gamma\); \(R=|\Gamma|^2\), \(T=1-R\). Identical to a [[id:transmission-lines][transmission-line]] mismatch with \(Z\to\eta\).

* Oblique incidence (derivation)
The tangential wavevector must be equal on both sides at every point of the interface: \(k_1\sin\theta_1=k_2\sin\theta_2\) (Snell) and \(\theta_r=\theta_i\). Matching tangential \(\mathbf E,\mathbf H\) separately for \(\perp\) (s, TE) and \(\parallel\) (p, TM) polarisation gives the coefficients in the summary (non-magnetic media), with \(t_\perp=1+r_\perp\), \(t_\parallel=(1+r_\parallel)\frac{n_1}{n_2}\). Power: \(R=|r|^2\), \(T=\frac{n_2\cos\theta_2}{n_1\cos\theta_1}|t|^2\).

* Total internal reflection
For \(n_1>n_2\) and \(\theta_1>\theta_c\): \(\cos\theta_2=-j\sqrt{\sin^2\theta_1/\sin^2\theta_c-1}\), \(|r|=1\) with a polarisation-dependent phase \(\phi\) (the basis of [[id:dielectric-slab-waveguide][waveguide modes]]). The transmitted field is evanescent, \(e^{-\alpha_2y}\), \(\alpha_2=k_2\sqrt{(n_1/n_2)^2\sin^2\theta_1-1}\): penetration depth \(\sim\lambda/2\pi\). Bringing a second medium within this depth gives frustrated TIR — optical tunnelling, the analogue of [[id:tunnelling-and-wkb][quantum tunnelling]].

* Worked example
Silicon (\(n=3.48\)) to air at 1550 nm: \(R=\left(\frac{2.48}{4.48}\right)^2=30.6\%\) per facet. A quarter-wave anti-reflection layer of \(n=\sqrt{3.48}=1.87\) (e.g. Si\(_3\)N\(_4\)-like), thickness \(\lambda/4n=207\) nm, cancels it. Critical angle Si/air: \(\arcsin(1/3.48)=16.7^\circ\) — why LEDs trap light ([[id:light-emitting-diodes][extraction efficiency]]).

* Engineering applications
Anti-reflection coatings on solar cells and lenses, Brewster windows in gas lasers, polarising beam splitters, prism couplers and ATR spectroscopy (evanescent sensing), fibre guidance by TIR, radar absorbers.

* Pitfall
Two conventions exist for the sign of \(r_\parallel\); they differ by \(\pi\) at normal incidence. Choose one and keep it in interference calculations.
