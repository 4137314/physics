#+title: Scattering parameters
#+summary: For an N-port, incident and reflected waves \(a_i=V_i^+/\sqrt{Z_0}\), \(b_i=V_i^-/\sqrt{Z_0}\) are related by \(\mathbf b=S\mathbf a\); \(|S_{11}|\) is the input reflection, \(|S_{21}|^2\) the transmitted power fraction; reciprocal networks have \(S=S^T\), lossless ones \(S^\dagger S=1\).

* Definition
\(S_{ij}=\left.\frac{b_i}{a_j}\right|_{a_k=0,\,k\neq j}\): drive port \(j\), terminate every other port in \(Z_0\) [cite:@kurokawa1965]. Power in the waves: \(|a|^2/2\) (peak phasors). Return loss \(-20\log|S_{11}|\), insertion loss \(-20\log|S_{21}|\).

* Properties (proof sketch)
- *Reciprocity* (\(\epsilon,\mu\) symmetric tensors, no ferrites or active bias): Lorentz reciprocity theorem gives \(S_{ij}=S_{ji}\).
- *Losslessness*: total outgoing power equals incoming for every \(\mathbf a\): \(\mathbf a^\dagger\mathbf a=\mathbf b^\dagger\mathbf b=\mathbf a^\dagger S^\dagger S\mathbf a\Rightarrow S^\dagger S=1\). Columns are orthonormal: e.g. a lossless reciprocal 3-port cannot be matched at all ports (hence resistive power dividers or circulators).
- Same structure as the quantum [[id:s-matrix-and-cross-sections][S matrix]] and the [[id:beam-splitter-and-hong-ou-mandel][beam splitter]] unitary.

* Conversions
Shifting reference planes by \(\theta_i=\beta\ell_i\): \(S'_{ij}=S_{ij}e^{-j(\theta_i+\theta_j)}\). Cascades are easier with ABCD or T-matrices; \(S_{21}=\frac{2}{A+B/Z_0+CZ_0+D}\).

* Worked example
Ideal 3 dB 90° hybrid: \(S=\frac{-1}{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&j&1&0\\j&0&0&1\\1&0&0&j\\0&1&j&0\end{pmatrix}\). Check unitarity: each column has two entries of magnitude \(1/\sqrt2\), and columns 1, 2 are orthogonal: \(\bar{(j)}\cdot0+\dots=0\). Port 1 splits equally to ports 2 and 3 with 90° phase difference; port 4 is isolated — the microwave twin of the optical [[id:directional-couplers][directional coupler]].

* Engineering applications
Vector network analyser measurement and calibration (SOLT, TRL), amplifier stability (\(K>1\), \(|\Delta|<1\)) and gain circles, filters and duplexers, Touchstone files for SPICE/EM co-simulation, photonic circuit simulation (same formalism in [[id:silicon-photonic-waveguides][silicon photonics]]).

* Pitfall
S-parameters are defined for a reference impedance; changing \(Z_0\) (e.g. 50 → 75 Ω) changes every \(S_{ij}\), even though the device is the same.
