#+title: Dispersion, group velocity and group index
#+summary: In a dispersive medium \(n(\lambda)\) varies: a pulse travels at \(v_g=\dd\omega/\dd k=c/N_g\) with group index \(N_g=n-\lambda\frac{\dd n}{\dd\lambda}\); Sellmeier \(n^2=1+\sum\frac{A_i\lambda^2}{\lambda^2-\lambda_i^2}\) and Cauchy \(n=A+B/\lambda^2+\dots\) fit real materials; absorption enters as \(n-j\kappa\), \(\alpha=4\pi\kappa/\lambda\).

* Definition
Phase velocity \(v_p=\omega/k=c/n\). A pulse is a packet of frequencies; its envelope moves at
\[ v_g=\frac{\dd\omega}{\dd k},\qquad N_g=\frac{c}{v_g}=n-\lambda\frac{\dd n}{\dd\lambda}=n+\omega\frac{\dd n}{\dd\omega}. \]
Derivation: \(k=n\omega/c\Rightarrow\frac{\dd k}{\dd\omega}=\frac1c\left(n+\omega\frac{\dd n}{\dd\omega}\right)\), and \(\omega\frac{\dd}{\dd\omega}=-\lambda\frac{\dd}{\dd\lambda}\).

* Origin: Lorentz oscillator
Bound electrons as damped oscillators driven by \(E\): \(\epsilon_r(\omega)=1+\frac{Ne^2}{\epsilon_0m}\frac{1}{\omega_0^2-\omega^2+j\gamma\omega}\). Below resonance \(n\) rises with \(\omega\) (normal dispersion); near \(\omega_0\) absorption peaks. Several resonances → Sellmeier. Real and imaginary parts are linked by Kramers–Kronig (causality).

* Worked example
Fused silica Sellmeier (\(A_1=0.6962\), \(\lambda_1=0.0684\) µm; \(A_2=0.4079\), \(\lambda_2=0.1162\); \(A_3=0.8975\), \(\lambda_3=9.896\)) at 1.55 µm: \(n^2=1+0.6962\cdot1.00195+0.4079\cdot1.00566+0.8975\cdot\frac{2.4025}{2.4025-97.93}=1+0.6976+0.4102-0.0226=2.0852\), \(n=1.444\), \(N_g\approx1.463\). Group delay 4.88 ns/m.

\(N_g\) has a minimum near 1.3 µm: there the group delay does not depend on \(\lambda\) and the material dispersion \(D_m\propto\dd N_g/\dd\lambda\) vanishes — the origin of the 1310 nm zero-dispersion window.

* Engineering applications
Pulse timing in fibres (\(L/v_g\)), chromatic dispersion and its compensation, prism compressors for ultrafast lasers, slow light in photonic crystals (\(N_g\sim30\)–100), group index of silicon waveguides (\(N_g\approx4.2\)) which sets the free spectral range of [[id:ring-resonators][ring resonators]].

* Exam-style exercise
Using the silica Sellmeier curve: (a) \(n\) and \(N_g\) at 1.3 µm. (b) Phase and group delays over 1 km. (c) Material dispersion at 0.85, 1.3 and 1.55 µm.
** Solution
(a) \(n=1.4469\), \(N_g=1.4616\). (b) \(nL/c=4826\) ns, \(N_gL/c=4875\) ns: the pulse arrives 49 ns after a phase front would. (c) \(D_m=-\frac\lambda c\frac{\dd^2n}{\dd\lambda^2}\): ≈ −84, +3 and +22 ps/(nm km). Adding waveguide dispersion (≈ −5 ps/(nm km)) gives the standard fibre's zero at 1310 nm and 17 at 1550 nm ([[id:fiber-dispersion-and-attenuation][fibre dispersion]]).

* Pitfall
Group index can exceed, or (anomalous dispersion) fall below, the phase index; neither \(v_p>c\) nor \(v_g>c\) near absorption lines violates causality — signal velocity stays \(\le c\).
