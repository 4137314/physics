#+title: Optical amplifiers: EDFA and SOA
#+summary: An erbium-doped fibre pumped at 980 or 1480 nm amplifies the whole C band (1530–1565 nm) with 20–40 dB gain; saturation output \(P_{sat}\) limits power, and amplified spontaneous emission sets a noise figure \(NF\approx2n_{sp}\ge3\) dB; SOAs are compact and electrically pumped but fast-saturating and polarisation dependent.

* EDFA model
Er\(^{3+}\) levels: \(^4I_{15/2}\) (ground), \(^4I_{13/2}\) (metastable, \(\tau\approx10\) ms), \(^4I_{11/2}\) (980 nm pump band, fast decay). Three-level system: the metastable level is inverted by the pump; long \(\tau\) makes gain dynamics slow (ms), so no inter-channel crosstalk at Gb/s. Gain \(G=\exp\int_0^L[\sigma_eN_2-\sigma_aN_1]\Gamma\,\dd z\).

* Noise
Each amplifier adds ASE with spectral density \(S_{ASE}=n_{sp}h\nu(G-1)\) per polarisation, \(n_{sp}=\frac{N_2}{N_2-N_1}\ge1\). Signal–ASE beat noise at the detector gives \(NF=\frac{\mathrm{SNR}_{in}}{\mathrm{SNR}_{out}}\approx2n_{sp}\) for large \(G\): quantum limit 3 dB. Chained amplifiers: OSNR \(\approx58+P_{ch}-\mathrm{span\ loss}-NF-10\log N\) dB (0.1 nm bandwidth, 1550 nm).

* SOA
A laser diode without feedback (AR-coated facets). Gain recovery ~100 ps: patterning at high bit rates but useful for all-optical switching (cross-gain/cross-phase modulation). NF 6–9 dB.

* Worked example
80 km spans of 0.22 dB/km (17.6 dB loss) with EDFAs of NF 5 dB, 0 dBm per channel, 10 spans: OSNR \(\approx58+0-17.6-5-10=25.4\) dB, enough for 10 Gb/s OOK (needs ~15 dB) and for 100 Gb/s coherent QPSK (~12–15 dB).

Pumping at 980 nm reaches an upper band that decays in µs to the metastable level (low noise, full inversion possible); 1480 nm pumps the top of the \(^4I_{13/2}\) band directly (higher efficiency, less inversion).

* Engineering applications
Long-haul and submarine DWDM links (amplifiers every 50–100 km), booster and preamplifiers, fibre lasers (Er, Yb), Raman amplification using the transmission fibre itself, SOAs in passive optical networks and photonic integrated circuits.

* Exam-style exercise
An EDFA amplifies a −30 dBm signal at 1550 nm by 30 dB with a 980 nm pump. (a) Output power. (b) Maximum (quantum-limited) pump-to-signal efficiency and the minimum pump for 10 mW output. (c) With NF = 5 dB, OSNR degradation of the signal.
** Solution
(a) 0 dBm = 1 mW. (b) Each pump photon yields at most one signal photon: \(\eta_{max}=\lambda_p/\lambda_s=980/1550=63\%\) → ≥ 15.8 mW of absorbed pump for 10 mW. (c) The electrical SNR after the amplifier is 5 dB worse than before it (shot-noise-limited input); a chain of \(N\) identical spans adds \(10\log N\) dB more.

* Pitfall
The noise figure is defined against a shot-noise-limited input; an amplifier cannot improve SNR, it only overcomes the thermal noise of the receiver that follows.
