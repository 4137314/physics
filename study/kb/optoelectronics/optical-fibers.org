#+title: Optical fibres: modes, numerical aperture and single-mode condition
#+summary: A step-index fibre (core radius \(a\), indices \(n_1>n_2\), \(\Delta=\frac{n_1-n_2}{n_1}\)) accepts light within \(\mathrm{NA}=\sin\theta_{max}=\sqrt{n_1^2-n_2^2}\); its weakly guiding modes are LP\(_{lm}\), the number is \(M\approx V^2/2\) with \(V=\frac{2\pi a}{\lambda}\mathrm{NA}\), and it is single-mode for \(V<2.405\); the fundamental mode is nearly Gaussian with MFD \(2w\approx2a(0.65+1.619V^{-3/2}+2.879V^{-6})\).

* Statement
- *Acceptance*: TIR at the core/cladding interface for rays inside the cone \(n_0\sin\theta_a\le\mathrm{NA}\) [cite:@kao1966].
- *Modes*: solving the wave equation in cylindrical coordinates, Bessel \(J_l\) in the core and \(K_l\) in the cladding; for \(\Delta\ll1\) (weak guidance) modes group into linearly polarised LP\(_{lm}\). LP\(_{01}\) has no cutoff; LP\(_{11}\) appears at \(V=2.405\), the first zero of \(J_0\) [cite:@snitzer1961].
- *Cutoff wavelength* \(\lambda_c=\frac{2\pi a\,\mathrm{NA}}{2.405}\): single-mode above it.

* Graded index
\(n(r)=n_1\sqrt{1-2\Delta(r/a)^\gamma}\); for \(\gamma\approx2\) (parabolic) rays follow sinusoidal paths with nearly equal transit times: intermodal spread drops from \(\frac{L n_1\Delta}{c}\) (step) to \(\frac{Ln_1\Delta^2}{8c}\). Number of modes \(M\approx V^2/4\).

* Worked example
SMF-28-like fibre: \(a=4.1\) µm, \(n_1=1.4504\), \(n_2=1.4447\): NA \(=\sqrt{2.10366-2.08716}=0.128\); at 1550 nm \(V=\frac{2\pi\cdot4.1}{1.55}\cdot0.128=2.13<2.405\) → single mode; \(\lambda_c=\frac{2\pi\cdot4.1\cdot0.128}{2.405}=1.37\) µm. MFD \(=8.2(0.65+1.619\cdot2.13^{-1.5}+2.879\cdot2.13^{-6})=8.2(0.65+0.521+0.031)=9.9\) µm. Multimode 50 µm core, NA 0.2 at 850 nm: \(V=37\), \(M\approx V^2/2\approx680\) (step) or ~340 (graded).

* Engineering applications
Long-haul (single-mode, 1550 nm) vs datacenter (multimode OM3/OM4 with VCSELs at 850 nm) links, fibre lasers and amplifiers, fibre sensors (gyroscopes, Bragg-grating strain/temperature gauges), hollow-core photonic-crystal fibres (low latency, high power), endoscopy.

* Exam-style exercise
A step-index fibre has \(n_1=1.46\) and a measured full acceptance angle of 30° in air; core diameter 50 µm. (a) NA and \(n_2\). (b) \(V\) and number of modes at 850 nm. (c) Wavelength above which it is single mode. (d) Would a graded-index version carry more or fewer modes?
** Solution
(a) \(\mathrm{NA}=\sin15^\circ=0.259\), \(n_2=\sqrt{1.46^2-0.259^2}=1.437\). (b) \(V=\frac{2\pi\cdot25}{0.85}\cdot0.259=47.8\), \(M\approx V^2/2=1.1\times10^3\). (c) \(\lambda_c=\frac{2\pi\cdot25\cdot0.259}{2.405}=16.9\) µm: never in practice. (d) Fewer, \(\approx V^2/4\approx570\), with far less intermodal dispersion.

* Pitfall
\(V<2.405\) assumes a step profile; bending raises the effective cutoff, and a fibre just below cutoff is very bend-sensitive (bending loss grows as the mode spreads into the cladding).
