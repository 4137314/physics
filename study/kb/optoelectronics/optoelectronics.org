#+title: Optoelectronics: the map of the subject
#+summary: Wave optics (dispersion, Fresnel, Gaussian beams, interference, diffraction, coherence), dielectric waveguides and fibres (modes, dispersion, attenuation), semiconductor emitters (LEDs, lasers, amplifiers), detectors and solar cells, and modulators: the chain transmitter → channel → receiver of every optical link.
