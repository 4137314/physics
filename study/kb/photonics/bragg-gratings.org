#+title: Waveguide Bragg gratings
#+summary: A periodic perturbation of period \(\Lambda\) couples forward and backward modes when \(\lambda_B=2n_{eff}\Lambda\); coupled-mode theory gives peak reflectivity \(R=\tanh^2(\kappa L)\) and stopband width \(\Delta\lambda=\frac{\lambda_B^2}{\pi n_g}\sqrt{\kappa^2+(\pi/L)^2}\); a quarter-wave phase shift opens a narrow transmission peak (the DFB laser cavity).

* Coupled-mode derivation
Forward \(A(z)e^{-j\beta z}\) and backward \(B(z)e^{j\beta z}\) waves, coupling \(\kappa\) from the index modulation \(\Delta n\): \(\kappa\approx\frac{2\Delta n}{\lambda}\) for a square-wave corrugation [cite:@kogelnik1972]. With detuning \(\delta=\beta-\pi/\Lambda\):
\[ \frac{\dd A}{\dd z}=-j\kappa Be^{2j\delta z},\qquad \frac{\dd B}{\dd z}=j\kappa Ae^{-2j\delta z}. \]
Boundary conditions \(A(0)=1\), \(B(L)=0\) give \(r(\delta)\); at \(\delta=0\): \(r=-j\tanh(\kappa L)\). Inside the stopband (\(|\delta|<\kappa\)) waves decay — a 1D [[id:photonic-crystals][photonic band gap]], the same physics as electron band gaps ([[id:bloch-theorem-and-energy-bands][Bloch]]).

* Worked example
SOI strip with 10 nm sidewall corrugation: \(\Delta n_{eff}\approx0.01\) → \(\kappa\approx\frac{2\cdot0.01}{1.55}\) µm\(^{-1}\) \(=1.3\times10^4\) m\(^{-1}\). For \(R=99\%\): \(\tanh\kappa L=0.995\), \(\kappa L=3.0\), \(L=230\) µm. Period \(\Lambda=\frac{1.55}{2\cdot2.45}=316\) nm. Bandwidth \(\approx\frac{\lambda^2\kappa}{\pi n_g}=\frac{2.4\times10^{-12}\cdot1.3\times10^4}{\pi\cdot4.2}=2.4\) nm.

* Engineering applications
On-chip filters and mirrors, DFB and DBR laser cavities ([[id:semiconductor-laser-diodes][laser diodes]]), fibre Bragg grating sensors and dispersion compensators, contra-directional couplers (add–drop filters without FSR), apodised gratings to suppress sidelobes.

* Pitfall
Fabrication smooths small corrugations (lithography blurs features < 100 nm), so the realised \(\kappa\) is often much smaller than designed: measure and calibrate.
