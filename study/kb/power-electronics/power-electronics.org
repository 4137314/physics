#+title: Power semiconductor devices: the map of the subject
#+summary: Power devices block high voltage with thick, lightly doped drift regions and conduct high current with low loss; the trade-off between breakdown voltage and on-resistance (unipolar limit \(R_{on,sp}\propto V_{BR}^{2.5}\)), conductivity modulation in bipolar devices (pin, IGBT, thyristor) and wide-bandgap materials (SiC, GaN) define the field; converters switch them at kHz–MHz.

* Overview
| block      | notes                                                    |
|------------+----------------------------------------------------------|
| devices    | power diodes, MOSFETs and IGBTs, thyristors, SiC and GaN |
| converters | buck, boost, losses and the switching cycle              |

* Device map
| device     | voltage      | frequency   | carriers | typical use            |
|------------+--------------+-------------+----------+------------------------|
| Si MOSFET  | < 600 V      | 100 kHz–MHz | unipolar | supplies, motor drives |
| IGBT       | 600 V–6.5 kV | 1–50 kHz    | bipolar  | inverters, traction    |
| thyristor  | up to 10 kV  | 50–1000 Hz  | bipolar  | HVDC, soft starters    |
| SiC MOSFET | 650 V–3.3 kV | 10–200 kHz  | unipolar | EV inverters, PV       |
| GaN HEMT   | < 650 V      | 0.1–10 MHz  | unipolar | chargers, data centres |

* Engineering applications
Electric vehicles, renewable inverters, motor drives, HVDC links, switch-mode power supplies, induction heating.
