#+title: Feynman rules of QED in momentum space
#+summary: \(-i\mathcal M\) is read off a diagram: \(u,\bar u,v,\bar v\) for external fermions, \(\epsilon_\mu,\epsilon^*_\mu\) for photons, \(-ie\gamma^\mu\) per vertex, \(\frac{i(\slashed p+m)}{p^2-m^2}\) per internal fermion, \(\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{q^2}\) per internal photon, \(\int\frac{\dd^4k}{(2\pi)^4}\) per loop, \(-1\) per fermion loop and relative signs for exchanged fermions.

* Rules (Feynman gauge, electron charge \(-e\))
| element                    | factor                                 |
|----------------------------+----------------------------------------|
| incoming / outgoing e⁻     | u(p,s) / ū(p,s)                        |
| incoming / outgoing e⁺     | v̄(p,s) / v(p,s)                       |
| incoming / outgoing photon | ε_μ(k) / ε*_μ(k)                       |
| vertex                     | −ieγ^μ, momentum conserved             |
| internal electron          | i(p̸ + m)/(p² − m² + iε)               |
| internal photon            | −i g_μν/(q² + iε)                      |
| loop                       | ∫ d⁴k/(2π)⁴, and −1 for a fermion loop |
Follow each fermion line *against* the arrow when writing spinors and matrices. Diagrams differing by exchange of identical external fermions get a relative minus sign.

* Derivation sketch
Expand \(S=T\exp(-i\int\mathcal H_I)\) with \(\mathcal H_I=e\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi A_\mu\) to order \(n\); Wick's theorem pairs fields into propagators (contractions) and external states; integrating vertex positions gives momentum conservation at each vertex [cite:@feynman1949;@dyson1949]. The \(1/n!\) cancels against the permutations of vertices.

* Crossing symmetry
Moving a particle from initial to final state turns it into its antiparticle with \(p\to-p\): \(e^-\mu^-\to e^-\mu^-\) (t-channel) and \(e^+e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-\) (s-channel) have the same \(\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}\) with \(s\leftrightarrow t\).

* Worked example
\(e^-\mu^-\to e^-\mu^-\): \(i\mathcal M=\bar u(p_3)(-ie\gamma^\mu)u(p_1)\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{q^2}\bar u(p_4)(-ie\gamma^\nu)u(p_2)\). Spin-averaged, high energy: \(\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=2e^4\frac{s^2+u^2}{t^2}\). Crossing \(s\leftrightarrow t\): \(2e^4\frac{t^2+u^2}{s^2}=e^4(1+\cos^2\theta)\) for \(e^+e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-\).

* Engineering applications
Monte Carlo event generators for detector design, radiation transport codes (Geant4) that tabulate QED cross sections, Feynman-diagram reasoning in condensed matter (electron–phonon diagrams for mobility and superconductivity).

* Pitfall
Polarisation sums: \(\sum\epsilon_\mu\epsilon^*_\nu\to-g_{\mu\nu}\) is valid only for gauge-invariant (current-conserving) amplitudes with all diagrams included.
