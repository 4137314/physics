#+title: Fine structure and the Zeeman effect
#+summary: Relativistic kinetic correction plus spin–orbit plus Darwin term give \(E_{nj}=E_n\left[1+\frac{\alpha^2}{n^2}\left(\frac{n}{j+1/2}-\frac34\right)\right]\); a weak field shifts levels by \(\mu_Bg_JBm_j\) with the Landé factor \(g_J=1+\frac{j(j+1)+s(s+1)-l(l+1)}{2j(j+1)}\).

* Spin–orbit
In the electron's rest frame the nucleus moves and produces \(\mathbf B=-\mathbf v\times\mathbf E/c^2\); with Thomas precession (factor ½) [cite:@uhlenbeck1925;@thomas1926]:
\[ H_{SO}=\frac{1}{2m^2c^2}\frac1r\frac{\dd V}{\dd r}\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S. \]
\(\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S\) is diagonal in \(\ket{l,s;j,m_j}\): first-order perturbation theory in the *coupled* basis.

* Fine structure
Adding \(-p^4/8m^3c^2\) and the Darwin term (for \(l=0\)), all three combine to [cite:@sommerfeld1916]
\[ \Delta E_{nj}=-\frac{mc^2\alpha^4}{2n^4}\left(\frac{n}{j+\tfrac12}-\frac34\right), \]
which depends only on \(j\): \(2s_{1/2}\) and \(2p_{1/2}\) stay degenerate (exactly as the [[id:dirac-equation][Dirac equation]] predicts; the [[id:lamb-shift][Lamb shift]] breaks it). \(n=2\): \(2p_{3/2}-2p_{1/2}=45\) µeV (10.9 GHz).

* Zeeman effect
\(H_Z=\frac{\mu_B}{\hbar}(\mathbf L+2\mathbf S)\cdot\mathbf B\) [cite:@zeeman1897].
- *Weak field* (\(\mu_BB\ll\) fine structure): project onto \(\mathbf J\) (projection theorem), \(\Delta E=g_J\mu_BBm_j\).
- *Strong field* (Paschen–Back): \(\Delta E=\mu_BB(m_l+2m_s)\), then add spin–orbit as a correction.

* Worked example
Sodium D lines, \(B=1\) T: \(3p_{1/2}\): \(g_J=2/3\); \(3s_{1/2}\): \(g_J=2\). \(\mu_BB=57.9\) µeV. The D1 line splits into 4 components with shifts \(\pm\tfrac23\mu_BB\), \(\pm\tfrac43\mu_BB\) — the anomalous Zeeman pattern.

* Engineering applications
Zeeman-tuned atomic magnetometers, magneto-optical traps, laser frequency stabilisation (DAVLL), Zeeman slowers, clock transitions chosen with \(m=0\) to be field-insensitive at first order.

* Pitfall
Using \(g_J=1\) (orbital only) or \(g_s=1\) misses the factor 2 of spin; the electron \(g_s=2.0023\) ([[id:anomalous-magnetic-moment][g−2]]).
