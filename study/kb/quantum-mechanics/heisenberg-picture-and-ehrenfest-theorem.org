#+title: Heisenberg picture and Ehrenfest's theorem
#+summary: Moving the time dependence onto operators, \(A_H(t)=U^\dagger AU\), gives \(\dd A_H/\dd t=\frac{1}{i\hbar}[A_H,H]\); for \(H=p^2/2m+V\) the expectation values obey Newton's law \(m\,\dd^2\braket x/\dd t^2=-\braket{V'(x)}\).

* Statement
Schrödinger picture: states move, operators fixed. Heisenberg picture: \(\ket\psi_H=\ket{\psi(0)}\), \(A_H(t)=U^\dagger(t)A_SU(t)\) [cite:@heisenberg1925]. Same predictions because \(\bra{\psi(t)}A_S\ket{\psi(t)}=\bra{\psi(0)}A_H(t)\ket{\psi(0)}\). Differentiating \(U^\dagger AU\) with \(i\hbar\dot U=HU\):
\[ \frac{\dd A_H}{\dd t}=\frac{1}{i\hbar}[A_H,H]\;\left(+\frac{\partial A}{\partial t}\right)_H. \]
This mirrors the classical \(\dot A=\{A,H\}\) under \(\{\,,\}\to[\,,]/i\hbar\) (Dirac).

* Ehrenfest theorem
With \(H=p^2/2m+V(x)\), using \([x,F(p)]=i\hbar F'(p)\) and \([p,G(x)]=-i\hbar G'(x)\) [cite:@ehrenfest1927]:
\[ \frac{\dd x_H}{\dd t}=\frac{p_H}{m},\qquad \frac{\dd p_H}{\dd t}=-V'(x_H)\;\Rightarrow\; m\frac{\dd^2\braket{x}}{\dd t^2}=-\braket{V'(x)}. \]
The centre of a packet follows the classical trajectory only when \(\braket{V'(x)}\simeq V'(\braket x)\), i.e. when \(V\) varies little over the packet (exact for \(V\) at most quadratic).

* Interaction picture
Split \(H=H_0+V(t)\): \(\ket\psi_I=e^{iH_0t/\hbar}\ket\psi_S\), \(V_I=e^{iH_0t/\hbar}Ve^{-iH_0t/\hbar}\), \(i\hbar\partial_t\ket\psi_I=V_I\ket\psi_I\). Base of time-dependent perturbation theory and of the S-matrix of [[id:s-matrix-and-cross-sections][QED]].

* Worked example
Free particle: \(x_H(t)=x(0)+p(0)t/m\). Then \([x_H(t),x_H(0)]=-i\hbar t/m\) and, by the [[id:uncertainty-relation][uncertainty relation]], \(\Delta x(t)\Delta x(0)\ge\hbar t/2m\): every packet must eventually spread. An electron packet of 1 nm doubles its width in \(t\sim2m\Delta x^2/\hbar\approx17\) fs.

* Pitfall
Constants of motion in the Heisenberg picture (\([A,H]=0\)) are operators that do not change; it does not mean the state is an eigenstate of \(A\).
