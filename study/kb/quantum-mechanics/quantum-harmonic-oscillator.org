#+title: The quantum harmonic oscillator
#+summary: With \(a=\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}}\left(x+\frac{ip}{m\omega}\right)\), \([a,a^\dagger]=1\) and \(H=\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+\tfrac12)\), so \(E_n=\hbar\omega(n+\tfrac12)\), \(a^\dagger\ket n=\sqrt{n+1}\ket{n+1}\), \(a\ket n=\sqrt n\ket{n-1}\); the same algebra quantises light, phonons and LC circuits.

* Statement
\(H=\frac{p^2}{2m}+\frac12m\omega^2x^2\) [cite:@dirac1927]. Define \(a\) as above and \(N=a^\dagger a\). Then
\[ [a,a^\dagger]=1,\quad H=\hbar\omega\left(N+\tfrac12\right),\quad [N,a]=-a,\quad [N,a^\dagger]=a^\dagger. \]
Spectrum \(E_n=\hbar\omega(n+\tfrac12)\), \(n=0,1,2,\dots\), \(\ket n=\frac{(a^\dagger)^n}{\sqrt{n!}}\ket0\) [cite:@heisenberg1925].

* Proof
1. \(a^\dagger a=\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}\left(x^2+\frac{p^2}{m^2\omega^2}\right)+\frac{i}{2\hbar}[x,p]=\frac{H}{\hbar\omega}-\frac12\).
2. \(N(a\ket n)=(aN-a)\ket n=(n-1)a\ket n\): \(a\) lowers \(n\) by one, \(a^\dagger\) raises it.
3. \(\|a\ket n\|^2=\bra nN\ket n=n\ge0\). Lowering repeatedly from a non-integer \(n\) would reach a negative norm, impossible; so the sequence must stop at \(a\ket0=0\), forcing \(n\in\mathbb N\).
4. Normalisation: \(a\ket n=\sqrt n\ket{n-1}\), \(a^\dagger\ket n=\sqrt{n+1}\ket{n+1}\).
5. Ground state from \(a\ket0=0\): \((x+\frac{\hbar}{m\omega}\partial_x)\psi_0=0\Rightarrow\psi_0=\left(\frac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar}\right)^{1/4}e^{-m\omega x^2/2\hbar}\); higher states are Hermite functions \(\psi_n\propto H_n(\xi)e^{-\xi^2/2}\), \(\xi=x\sqrt{m\omega/\hbar}\).

* Useful matrix elements
\(x=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}(a+a^\dagger)\), \(p=i\sqrt{\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}}(a^\dagger-a)\). In \(\ket n\): \(\braket x=\braket p=0\), \(\braket{x^2}=\frac{\hbar}{m\omega}(n+\tfrac12)\), so \(\Delta x\Delta p=\hbar(n+\tfrac12)\). Heisenberg picture: \(a(t)=a\,e^{-i\omega t}\).

* Worked example
Vibration of CO, \(\tilde\nu=2143\) cm\(^{-1}\): \(\hbar\omega=0.266\) eV. At 300 K, \(k_BT=0.026\) eV, so the population of \(n=1\) is \(e^{-10.3}\approx3\times10^{-5}\): molecules sit in the ground state, and IR absorption is the \(0\to1\) line (selection rule \(\Delta n=\pm1\) because \(x\propto a+a^\dagger\)).

* Engineering applications
- *Quantised LC circuit*: \(H=\frac{Q^2}{2C}+\frac{\Phi^2}{2L}\) with \([\Phi,Q]=i\hbar\): microwave resonators of circuit QED, the transmon as an anharmonic oscillator.
- *Phonons* (heat capacity, thermal conductivity), *photons* ([[id:quantization-of-the-electromagnetic-field][field quantisation]]), trapped-ion motional modes.
- Zero-point fluctuations set the vacuum noise floor of amplifiers (\(\hbar\omega/2\) per mode).

* Pitfall
The ground state is not at rest: \(E_0=\hbar\omega/2\neq0\) and \(\Delta x\neq0\). Also \(a\) is not Hermitian: it is not an observable.
