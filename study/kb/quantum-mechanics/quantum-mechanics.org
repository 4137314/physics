#+title: Quantum mechanics: the map of the subject
#+summary: States are unit vectors (rays) \(\ket{\psi}\) of a complex Hilbert space, observables are self-adjoint operators, probabilities are \(|\braket{a|\psi}|^2\), and time evolution is the unitary group generated by the Hamiltonian; everything else in the course is a consequence or an application of these four rules.

* Overview
The domain follows the logic of a graduate course (Sakurai–Napolitano), with the rigorous foundations (Moretti, Landsman, Woit, Haag) as a final layer.

| block         | notes                                                       |
|---------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
| postulates    | Stern–Gerlach, kets, observables, measurement, commutators  |
| dynamics      | Schrödinger and Heisenberg pictures, propagator, gauge      |
| 1D systems    | wells, tunnelling and WKB, harmonic oscillator              |
| ang. momentum | algebra, orbital, hydrogen, addition, symmetries            |
| approximation | perturbation (static, time-dependent), variational          |
| many bodies   | identical particles, scattering                             |
| q. info       | density operator, entanglement, Bell, qubits                |
| foundations   | self-adjoint operators, Stone, representations, C*-algebras |

* The four postulates in one table
| postulate   | object                 | formula                            |
|-------------+------------------------+------------------------------------|
| state       | ray in Hilbert space H | ψ ∈ H, ‖ψ‖ = 1, ψ ~ e^{iα}ψ        |
| observable  | self-adjoint operator  | A = A† = Σ a P_a                   |
| measurement | Born rule, projection  | p(a) = ⟨ψ, P_a ψ⟩, ψ → P_aψ/‖P_aψ‖ |
| dynamics    | unitary group          | U(t) = e^{−iHt/ħ}, iħ∂ψ/∂t = Hψ    |

* How to study it
Follow the prerequisite order (`]`). For every note: state the result from memory, redo the proof on paper, then solve the worked example without looking. The [[id:exam-practice-quantum-mechanics][exam practice]] collects full problems; the [[id:formula-sheet-quantum][formula sheet]] is for the last day.
