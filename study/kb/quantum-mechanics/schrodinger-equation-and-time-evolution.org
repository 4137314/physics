#+title: The Schrödinger equation and time evolution
#+summary: Time evolution is a unitary \(U(t)\) with \(i\hbar\,\partial_tU=HU\); for time-independent \(H\), \(U=e^{-iHt/\hbar}\) and expanding in energy eigenkets \(\ket\psi(t)=\sum_E c_Ee^{-iEt/\hbar}\ket E\); in position space \(i\hbar\partial_t\psi=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\psi+V\psi\) with conserved probability current.

* Statement
Requirements on \(U(t,t_0)\): unitarity (probability conserved), composition \(U(t_2,t_0)=U(t_2,t_1)U(t_1,t_0)\), continuity. Then \(U(t_0+\dd t,t_0)=1-iH\dd t/\hbar\) with \(H\) Hermitian, and
\[ i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}U(t,t_0)=H\,U(t,t_0),\qquad i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\ket{\psi(t)}=H\ket{\psi(t)}. \]

* Derivation of the solution
1. \(H\) time independent: \(U=e^{-iH(t-t_0)/\hbar}\).
2. Expand \(\ket{\psi(0)}=\sum_E\ket E\braket{E|\psi(0)}\); since \(e^{-iHt/\hbar}\ket E=e^{-iEt/\hbar}\ket E\),
\[ \ket{\psi(t)}=\sum_E c_E\,e^{-iEt/\hbar}\ket E. \]
3. *Stationary states*: if \(\ket\psi=\ket E\), all expectations are time independent. For any observable \(B\) commuting with \(H\), \(\braket B\) is constant.
4. Position space with \(H=p^2/2m+V(x)\): \(\bra x p^2\ket\psi=-\hbar^2\nabla^2\psi\), giving the wave equation.

* Probability current
From the equation and its conjugate, \(\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0\) with
\[ \rho=|\psi|^2,\qquad \mathbf j=\frac{\hbar}{m}\,\mathrm{Im}\left(\psi^*\nabla\psi\right). \]
For a plane wave \(\psi=Ae^{ikx}\), \(j=|A|^2\hbar k/m=|A|^2v\): density times velocity. This current gives transmission coefficients in [[id:tunnelling-and-wkb][tunnelling]].

* Energy–time and beats
A superposition of two levels \(E_1,E_2\) gives expectations oscillating at \(\omega_{21}=(E_2-E_1)/\hbar\): the "Bohr frequency" radiated in a transition. Neutrino oscillations and spin precession are such beats.

* Worked example
An electron prepared in \(\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(\ket1+\ket2)\) of a 10 nm infinite well: \(E_n=n^2\cdot3.76\) meV, so \(E_2-E_1=11.3\) meV and the packet sloshes with frequency \(\nu=2.7\) THz — the principle of quantum-well THz emitters and quantum cascade lasers.

* Pitfall
\(U=e^{-iHt/\hbar}\) holds only if \(H\) is time independent; for \([H(t_1),H(t_2)]\neq0\) one needs the time-ordered (Dyson) series, see [[id:time-dependent-perturbation-and-golden-rule][time-dependent perturbation]].
