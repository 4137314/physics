#+title: Time-independent perturbation theory
#+summary: For \(H=H_0+\lambda V\), \(E_n=E_n^{(0)}+V_{nn}+\sum_{k\neq n}\frac{|V_{kn}|^2}{E_n^{(0)}-E_k^{(0)}}+\dots\) and \(\ket n=\ket{n^0}+\sum_{k\neq n}\frac{V_{kn}}{E_n^{(0)}-E_k^{(0)}}\ket{k^0}\); with degeneracy, first diagonalise \(V\) inside the degenerate subspace.

* Method (non-degenerate)
Expand \(\ket n=\ket{n^0}+\lambda\ket{n^1}+\dots\), \(E_n=E^{(0)}_n+\lambda\Delta^{(1)}+\lambda^2\Delta^{(2)}\); impose \((H_0+\lambda V)\ket n=E_n\ket n\) order by order, with \(\braket{n^0|n}=1\).
- Order 1, project on \(\bra{n^0}\): \(\Delta^{(1)}=V_{nn}=\bra{n^0}V\ket{n^0}\).
- Order 1, project on \(\bra{k^0}\): \(\braket{k^0|n^1}=\frac{V_{kn}}{E_n^0-E_k^0}\).
- Order 2: \(\Delta^{(2)}=\sum_{k\neq n}\frac{|V_{kn}|^2}{E^0_n-E^0_k}\).
Validity: \(|V_{kn}|\ll|E^0_n-E^0_k|\). The ground-state second-order shift is always negative: levels repel.

* Degenerate case
If \(E^0\) is \(g\)-fold degenerate, the denominators vanish. Diagonalise the \(g\times g\) matrix \(\bra{m^0}V\ket{m'^0}\) in the degenerate subspace: its eigenvalues are the first-order shifts and its eigenvectors the correct zeroth-order states.

* Worked example
Two-level system \(H=\begin{pmatrix}E_1&\Delta\\\Delta&E_2\end{pmatrix}\): exact \(E_\pm=\frac{E_1+E_2}2\pm\sqrt{\left(\frac{E_1-E_2}{2}\right)^2+\Delta^2}\). Expanding for \(\Delta\ll|E_1-E_2|\): \(E_1-\Delta^2/(E_2-E_1)\), the second-order formula. At \(E_1=E_2\) (degenerate): \(E\pm\Delta\), the degenerate-theory result — an *avoided crossing*.

* Quadratic Stark effect
Ground state of hydrogen in \(\mathbf E=E\hat z\), \(V=eEz\): first order vanishes by parity; second order \(\Delta=-\tfrac12\alpha_pE^2\) with polarisability \(\alpha_p=\frac92\cdot4\pi\epsilon_0a_0^3\). The \(n=2\) level is degenerate: \(2s\) and \(2p_0\) mix and split linearly, \(\pm3eEa_0\).

* Engineering applications
Quantum-confined Stark effect in quantum-well electro-absorption modulators (fast optical modulators for telecom); band-structure \(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf p\) theory giving effective masses ([[id:effective-mass-and-holes][effective mass]]); dispersive qubit read-out (second-order shift \(g^2/\Delta\)).

* Pitfall
Using non-degenerate formulas on degenerate levels gives infinities or wrong states; always check degeneracy first.
