#+title: The Jaynes–Cummings model
#+summary: A two-level atom coupled to one cavity mode in the rotating-wave approximation, \(H=\hbar\omega_c a^\dagger a+\tfrac{\hbar\omega_a}{2}\sigma_z+\hbar g(a\sigma_++a^\dagger\sigma_-)\), splits into 2×2 blocks \(\{\ket{e,n},\ket{g,n+1}\}\) with Rabi frequency \(2g\sqrt{n+1}\); even the vacuum drives Rabi oscillations.

* Model
Dipole coupling \(-\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E\) with \(\mathbf E\propto a+a^\dagger\) and \(\mathbf d\propto\sigma_++\sigma_-\); dropping the energy-non-conserving terms \(a\sigma_-\), \(a^\dagger\sigma_+\) (RWA, \(g\ll\omega\)) leaves the JC Hamiltonian, which conserves \(N=a^\dagger a+\sigma_+\sigma_-\).

* Solution
In the block with \(n\) excitations, detuning \(\Delta=\omega_a-\omega_c\):
\[ H_n=\hbar\begin{pmatrix}\omega_c(n+\tfrac12)+\Delta/2 & g\sqrt{n+1}\\ g\sqrt{n+1} & \omega_c(n+\tfrac12)-\Delta/2\end{pmatrix}, \quad E_{n,\pm}=\hbar\omega_c(n+\tfrac12)\pm\frac\hbar2\sqrt{\Delta^2+4g^2(n+1)}. \]
On resonance the dressed states \(\ket{n,\pm}=(\ket{e,n}\pm\ket{g,n+1})/\sqrt2\) are split by \(2\hbar g\sqrt{n+1}\) (vacuum Rabi splitting \(2\hbar g\) for \(n=0\)). An excited atom in the empty cavity: \(P_e(t)=\cos^2(gt)\) — reversible exchange of one photon. With a coherent field the different \(\sqrt{n+1}\) frequencies dephase and rephase: collapse and revival, a direct proof of field quantisation.

* Dispersive regime
\(|\Delta|\gg g\): second-order perturbation gives \(H\approx\hbar(\omega_c+\chi\sigma_z)a^\dagger a+\tfrac\hbar2(\omega_a+\chi)\sigma_z\), \(\chi=g^2/\Delta\). The cavity frequency depends on the qubit state: non-destructive read-out of superconducting qubits.

* Worked example
Transmon–resonator: \(g/2\pi=100\) MHz, \(\Delta/2\pi=1\) GHz: \(\chi/2\pi=10\) MHz, larger than a resonator linewidth \(\kappa/2\pi\sim1\) MHz, so the two qubit states shift the resonator by \(2\chi\) — well resolved. Strong coupling requires \(g>\kappa,\gamma\).

* Engineering applications
Circuit QED (qubit read-out and two-qubit gates via a bus resonator), cavity-QED single-photon sources, polariton lasers, Rydberg-atom masers.

* Pitfall
The RWA fails in ultrastrong coupling (\(g/\omega\gtrsim0.1\)): the counter-rotating terms then shift and mix levels (Bloch–Siegert shift, Rabi model).
