#+title: Quantum optics: the map of the subject
#+summary: Each mode of the electromagnetic field is a harmonic oscillator; photons are its quanta, light states (Fock, coherent, squeezed, thermal) are distinguished by their correlation functions \(g^{(1)},g^{(2)}\), and atoms couple to modes through the Jaynes–Cummings Hamiltonian and decay through master equations.

* Overview
| block           | notes                                                  |
|-----------------+--------------------------------------------------------|
| field           | quantisation of the free field, Fock states            |
| states of light | coherent, squeezed, thermal                            |
| coherence       | Glauber correlations, beam splitter and HOM            |
| light–matter    | Jaynes–Cummings, spontaneous emission, master equation |

* Engineering applications
Single-photon sources and detectors for quantum communication, squeezed light in gravitational-wave detectors, cavity and circuit QED for quantum computing, laser linewidth and noise in optical communications ([[id:noise-in-photodetectors][shot noise]]).
