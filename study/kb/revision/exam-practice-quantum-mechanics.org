#+title: Exam practice: quantum mechanics (solved)
#+summary: Eight exam-style problems with complete solutions covering measurement, uncertainty, wells, oscillator, angular momentum, perturbation theory, golden rule and entanglement; try each one for 15–20 minutes before reading the solution.

* Problem 1 — measurement on spin ½
A spin is prepared in \(\ket\psi=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\ket++\sqrt{\frac23}\ket-\). (a) \(P(S_x=+\hbar/2)\)? (b) \(\braket{S_x}\), \(\Delta S_x\)? (c) After measuring \(S_x=+\hbar/2\), what is \(P(S_z=+\hbar/2)\)?
** Solution
(a) \(\ket{S_x;+}=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}(\ket++\ket-)\): amplitude \(\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(\frac1{\sqrt3}+\sqrt{\frac23}\right)=0.986\), \(P=0.971\). (b) \(\braket{S_x}=\frac\hbar2(2P-1)=0.471\hbar\); \(\braket{S_x^2}=\hbar^2/4\), \(\Delta S_x=\frac\hbar2\sqrt{1-(2P-1)^2}=0.167\hbar\). Check with \(\braket{S_x}=\hbar\,\mathrm{Re}(c_+^*c_-)=\hbar\frac{\sqrt2}{3}=0.471\hbar\) ✓. (c) State collapsed to \(\ket{S_x;+}\): \(P=1/2\).

* Problem 2 — infinite well, sudden expansion
An electron in the ground state of a well of width \(L\); the wall suddenly moves to \(2L\). Probability to find it in the ground state of the new well?
** Solution
\(c_1=\int_0^L\sqrt{\frac2L}\sin\frac{\pi x}{L}\sqrt{\frac1L}\sin\frac{\pi x}{2L}\dd x=\frac{\sqrt2}{L}\int_0^L\sin\frac{\pi x}L\sin\frac{\pi x}{2L}\dd x=\frac{\sqrt2}{L}\cdot\frac{4L}{3\pi}\), so \(|c_1|^2=\frac{32}{9\pi^2}=0.36\). The sudden approximation keeps the wave function and projects it on the new eigenstates.

* Problem 3 — harmonic oscillator expectation values
For \(\ket\psi=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}(\ket0+\ket1)\) find \(\braket{x}(t)\).
** Solution
\(x=\sqrt{\frac\hbar{2m\omega}}(a+a^\dagger)\); \(\bra0x\ket1=\sqrt{\frac\hbar{2m\omega}}\). With phases \(e^{-i\omega t/2},e^{-3i\omega t/2}\): \(\braket x(t)=2\cdot\frac12\sqrt{\frac\hbar{2m\omega}}\cos\omega t=\sqrt{\frac\hbar{2m\omega}}\cos\omega t\). Oscillates at \(\omega\) as Ehrenfest requires.

* Problem 4 — tunnelling estimate
An electron with \(E=1\) eV hits a 3 eV, 0.5 nm barrier. Estimate \(T\).
** Solution
\(\kappa=\sqrt{2m\cdot2\text{ eV}}/\hbar=7.24\) nm\(^{-1}\), \(\kappa a=3.62\), \(T\approx16\cdot\frac13\cdot\frac23e^{-7.24}=3.56\cdot7.2\times10^{-4}=2.6\times10^{-3}\). Exact formula: \(\left[1+\frac{9\sinh^2 3.62}{4\cdot1\cdot2}\right]^{-1}=\left[1+\frac{9\cdot348}{8}\right]^{-1}=2.5\times10^{-3}\) ✓.

* Problem 5 — angular momentum
For \(l=1\) compute the matrix of \(L_x\) in the \(\ket{1,m}\) basis and its eigenvalues.
** Solution
\(L_x=\frac12(L_++L_-)\), \(\bra{1,m+1}L_+\ket{1,m}=\hbar\sqrt2\) for \(m=-1,0\): \(L_x=\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}\), eigenvalues \(0,\pm\hbar\) (as for any axis, by rotational symmetry).

* Problem 6 — perturbation of the oscillator
\(H=\frac{p^2}{2m}+\frac12m\omega^2x^2+\lambda x\). Find \(E_n\) to second order and compare with the exact result.
** Solution
First order: \(\bra nx\ket n=0\). Second: only \(k=n\pm1\): \(|\bra{n+1}x\ket n|^2=\frac{\hbar(n+1)}{2m\omega}\), \(|\bra{n-1}x\ket n|^2=\frac{\hbar n}{2m\omega}\); \(E^{(2)}=\lambda^2\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}\left[\frac{n+1}{-\hbar\omega}+\frac{n}{\hbar\omega}\right]=-\frac{\lambda^2}{2m\omega^2}\). Exact: completing the square, \(E_n=\hbar\omega(n+\frac12)-\frac{\lambda^2}{2m\omega^2}\) ✓ (second order is exact).

* Problem 7 — Zeeman pattern
Hydrogen \(2p_{3/2}\) in \(B=0.5\) T: list the shifts.
** Solution
\(g_J=1+\frac{15/4+3/4-2}{2\cdot15/4}=\frac43\). \(\mu_BB=28.9\) µeV; shifts \(g_J\mu_BBm_j=\pm19.3,\pm57.9\) µeV for \(m_j=\pm\frac12,\pm\frac32\). Weak field holds because the fine-structure splitting (45 µeV) is comparable — at 0.5 T one is at the edge; for a rigorous answer diagonalise in the \(n=2\), \(l=1\) subspace.

* Problem 8 — Bell state measurement
For \(\ket{\Phi^+}=\frac1{\sqrt2}(\ket{00}+\ket{11})\) compute \(E(a,b)\) for measurements of \(\sigma_z\cos\theta+\sigma_x\sin\theta\) at angles \(\theta_a,\theta_b\), and the CHSH value for \(0,\pi/2,\pi/4,-\pi/4\).
** Solution
\(E=\cos(\theta_a-\theta_b)\) (for real states in the x–z plane). \(S=E(0,\frac\pi4)+E(0,-\frac\pi4)+E(\frac\pi2,\frac\pi4)-E(\frac\pi2,-\frac\pi4)=3\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}2-\left(-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)=2\sqrt2\).
