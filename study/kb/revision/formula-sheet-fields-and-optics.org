#+title: Formula sheet: fields, optoelectronics and photonics
#+summary: Maxwell, waves, lines and antennas; wave optics, fibres, sources and detectors; integrated-photonics components — the formulas used in exam problems, each derived in its note.

* Fields
\[ \nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho,\quad \nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0,\quad \nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B,\quad \nabla\times\mathbf H=\mathbf J+\partial_t\mathbf D,\quad \braket{\mathbf S}=\tfrac12\mathrm{Re}(\mathbf E\times\mathbf H^*) \]
\[ \gamma=\sqrt{j\omega\mu(\sigma+j\omega\epsilon)},\quad \eta=\sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\sigma+j\omega\epsilon}},\quad \delta=\sqrt{\frac{2}{\omega\mu\sigma}},\quad \eta_0=377\,\Omega \]
\[ r_\perp=\frac{n_1\cos\theta_1-n_2\cos\theta_2}{n_1\cos\theta_1+n_2\cos\theta_2},\quad r_\parallel=\frac{n_2\cos\theta_1-n_1\cos\theta_2}{n_2\cos\theta_1+n_1\cos\theta_2},\quad \tan\theta_B=\frac{n_2}{n_1},\quad \sin\theta_c=\frac{n_2}{n_1} \]

* Lines, waveguides, antennas
\[ Z_0=\sqrt{\frac{R+j\omega L}{G+j\omega C}},\quad \Gamma_L=\frac{Z_L-Z_0}{Z_L+Z_0},\quad Z_{in}=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0\tan\beta\ell}{Z_0+jZ_L\tan\beta\ell},\quad \mathrm{VSWR}=\frac{1+|\Gamma|}{1-|\Gamma|} \]
\[ f_{c,mn}=\frac{c}{2}\sqrt{\left(\frac ma\right)^2+\left(\frac nb\right)^2},\quad \lambda_g=\frac{\lambda}{\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}},\quad P_r=P_tG_tG_r\left(\frac{\lambda}{4\pi R}\right)^2,\quad A_e=\frac{\lambda^2G}{4\pi} \]

* Wave optics
\[ N_g=n-\lambda\frac{\dd n}{\dd\lambda},\quad z_0=\frac{\pi w_0^2}{\lambda},\quad \theta=\frac{\lambda}{\pi w_0},\quad \mathrm{FSR}=\frac{c}{2n_gL},\quad \mathcal F=\frac{\pi\sqrt R}{1-R},\quad l_c=\frac{\lambda^2}{\Delta\lambda} \]
\[ d(\sin\theta_m-\sin\theta_i)=m\lambda,\quad \theta_{Airy}=1.22\frac\lambda D,\quad R_{Bragg}=\left(\frac{1-(n_2/n_1)^{2N}}{1+(n_2/n_1)^{2N}}\right)^2 \]

* Fibres
\[ \mathrm{NA}=\sqrt{n_1^2-n_2^2},\quad V=\frac{2\pi a}{\lambda}\mathrm{NA}<2.405,\quad M\approx\frac{V^2}{2},\quad \Delta\tau=|D|L\Delta\lambda,\quad \alpha_{dB}=\frac{10}{L}\log\frac{P_{in}}{P_{out}} \]

* Sources and detectors
\[ \lambda_g[\mu m]=\frac{1.24}{E_g[eV]},\quad g_{th}=\alpha_i+\frac{1}{2L}\ln\frac{1}{R_1R_2},\quad P=\eta_d\frac{h\nu}{q}(I-I_{th}),\quad \Delta\nu_D=\frac{2\nu_0}{c}\sqrt{\frac{2k_BT\ln2}{M}} \]
\[ \mathcal R=\eta\frac{q\lambda}{hc},\quad \overline{i_s^2}=2qIB,\quad \overline{i_t^2}=\frac{4k_BTB}{R},\quad \mathrm{BER}=\tfrac12\mathrm{erfc}\frac{Q}{\sqrt2},\quad V_{oc}=\frac{nk_BT}q\ln\left(1+\frac{I_{sc}}{I_0}\right) \]

* Integrated photonics
\[ P_{cross}=\sin^2(\kappa L),\quad L_x=\frac{\lambda}{2\Delta n},\quad \mathrm{FSR}_{MZI}=\frac{\lambda^2}{n_g\Delta L},\quad T_{ring}=\frac{a^2-2ra\cos\phi+r^2}{1-2ar\cos\phi+(ar)^2},\quad \lambda_B=2n_{eff}\Lambda \]
\[ V_\pi=\frac{\lambda d}{n^3rL\Gamma},\quad \Delta n_{Si}=-8.8\times10^{-22}\Delta N-8.5\times10^{-18}\Delta P^{0.8},\quad F_P=\frac{3}{4\pi^2}\left(\frac\lambda n\right)^3\frac QV \]
