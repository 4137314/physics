* Method for top marks
1. *Understand*: read a note in study order (`]`), then close it and rederive the main result on paper.
2. *Compute*: redo the worked example with different numbers; check units and limits.
3. *Connect*: explain the engineering application and one link to another domain (e.g. the finite well ↔ slab waveguide ↔ quantum-well laser).
4. *Recall*: on later days use `r` (random note) and try to state the summary formula before reading.
5. *Exam*: two days before, solve the practice problems timed, then review the formula sheet.
