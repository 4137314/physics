#+title: Drift, diffusion and the Einstein relation
#+summary: Electron current \(J_n=qn\mu_nE+qD_n\frac{\dd n}{\dd x}\), hole current \(J_p=qp\mu_pE-qD_p\frac{\dd p}{\dd x}\); equilibrium (zero current, flat \(E_F\)) forces \(D/\mu=k_BT/q\); at high fields drift velocity saturates near \(10^7\) cm/s.

* Drift
Between collisions (mean time \(\tau\)) carriers accelerate: \(v_d=-\mu_nE\), \(\mu=q\tau/m^*\). Conductivity \(\sigma=q(n\mu_n+p\mu_p)\). Mobility falls with doping (impurity scattering) and temperature (phonons, \(\propto T^{-3/2}\)).

* Diffusion and Einstein relation (derivation)
Fick: flux \(-D\nabla n\) [cite:@einstein1905c;@roosbroeck1950]. In equilibrium with a built-in field, \(J_n=0\): \(qn\mu_nE=-qD_n\frac{\dd n}{\dd x}\). With \(n=N_ce^{-(E_c-E_F)/k_BT}\), \(E_F\) flat and \(E_c\) bending, \(\frac{\dd n}{\dd x}=-\frac{n}{k_BT}\frac{\dd E_c}{\dd x}\), and since \(\frac{\dd E_c}{\dd x}=qE\) (band edge is \(-q\) times the potential), \(\frac{\dd n}{\dd x}=-\frac{qnE}{k_BT}\). Substituting:
\[ \frac{D_n}{\mu_n}=\frac{D_p}{\mu_p}=\frac{k_BT}{q}=25.9\ \text{mV at 300 K}. \]
In general \(J_n=n\mu_n\frac{\dd E_{Fn}}{\dd x}\): current flows only where the quasi-Fermi level is tilted.

* Velocity saturation
At high field carriers emit optical phonons: \(v=\frac{\mu E}{1+\mu E/v_{sat}}\), \(v_{sat}\approx10^7\) cm/s in Si. Critical field \(E_c=v_{sat}/\mu\approx7\) kV/cm for electrons: reached in short-channel MOSFETs (1 V over 100 nm = 100 kV/cm), making \(I_D\) linear rather than quadratic in \(V_{GS}-V_T\).

* Worked example
n-Si with \(N_D=10^{16}\) cm\(^{-3}\), \(\mu_n=1200\) cm²/Vs: \(\rho=1/(1.6\times10^{-19}\cdot10^{16}\cdot1200)=0.52\) Ω cm. A diffused resistor of junction depth 1 µm has sheet resistance \(R_\square=\rho/t=5.2\) kΩ/□. \(D_n=0.0259\cdot1200=31\) cm²/s.

* Engineering applications
Resistor design in ICs (sheet resistance × squares), Hall sensors (\(R_H=1/qn\)) to measure carrier type and density, diffusion of minority carriers in bipolar transistors and solar cells, velocity-saturation limits in MOSFETs.

* Pitfall
The Einstein relation holds in non-degenerate equilibrium-like conditions; for degenerate doping \(D/\mu>k_BT/q\).
