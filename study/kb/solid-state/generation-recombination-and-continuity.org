#+title: Generation, recombination and the continuity equation
#+summary: Excess minority carriers obey \(\frac{\partial\Delta p}{\partial t}=D_p\frac{\partial^2\Delta p}{\partial x^2}-\mu_pE\frac{\partial\Delta p}{\partial x}-\frac{\Delta p}{\tau_p}+G\); they recombine radiatively (\(Bnp\)), through traps (Shockley–Read–Hall) or by Auger processes, and diffuse a distance \(L_p=\sqrt{D_p\tau_p}\) before recombining.

* Continuity equation
Conservation: \(\frac{\partial p}{\partial t}=-\frac1q\frac{\partial J_p}{\partial x}+G-R\). With the drift–diffusion current and low-level injection in n-type material (\(R-G_{th}=\Delta p/\tau_p\)), the equation in the summary follows.

* Recombination mechanisms
| mechanism   | rate                              | dominant in                    |
|-------------+-----------------------------------+--------------------------------|
| radiative   | B(np − n_i²)                      | direct gap (GaAs): LEDs        |
| SRH (traps) | (np − n_i²)/(τ_p(n+n₁)+τ_n(p+p₁)) | indirect gap (Si), defects     |
| Auger       | (C_n n + C_p p)(np − n_i²)        | high density: lasers, emitters |
| surface     | S·Δp at the surface               | solar cells, small devices     |
SRH is fastest for traps near mid-gap (\(n_1\approx p_1\approx n_i\)): gold and iron are lifetime killers.

* Diffusion length (derivation)
Steady state, no field, no generation for \(x>0\), \(\Delta p(0)\) fixed: \(D_p\Delta p''=\Delta p/\tau_p\Rightarrow\Delta p(x)=\Delta p(0)e^{-x/L_p}\), \(L_p=\sqrt{D_p\tau_p}\). Diffusion current at \(x=0\): \(qD_p\Delta p(0)/L_p\) — the building block of the [[id:pn-junction-diode-current][diode equation]].

* Worked example
Si, \(\tau_p=1\) µs, \(D_p=12\) cm²/s: \(L_p=\sqrt{12\times10^{-6}}\) cm \(=35\) µm. A solar-cell wafer of 180 µm needs \(L\gtrsim\) wafer thickness for good collection: \(\tau\sim\)100 µs–1 ms, i.e. very clean silicon. GaAs radiative lifetime at \(n=10^{18}\) cm\(^{-3}\): \(1/(Bn)=1/(7\times10^{-10}\cdot10^{18})\approx1.4\) ns.

* Engineering applications
Photoconductive detectors (gain \(=\tau/t_{transit}\)), LED internal quantum efficiency \(\eta=R_{rad}/R_{tot}\) ([[id:light-emitting-diodes][LEDs]]), solar-cell collection ([[id:solar-cells][solar cells]]), reverse-recovery time of power diodes (lifetime control by Pt/He implantation), DRAM retention (generation current).

* Pitfall
\(\tau\) depends on injection level and temperature; a single "lifetime" is only valid in low-level injection.
