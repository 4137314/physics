#+title: Intrinsic and doped semiconductors
#+summary: Intrinsic: \(n=p=n_i=\sqrt{N_cN_v}e^{-E_g/2k_BT}\); with fully ionised donors \(N_D\gg n_i\), \(n\simeq N_D\), \(p=n_i^2/N_D\) and \(E_c-E_F=k_BT\ln(N_c/N_D)\); in general neutrality \(n+N_A^-=p+N_D^+\) gives \(n=\frac{N_D-N_A}2+\sqrt{\left(\frac{N_D-N_A}2\right)^2+n_i^2}\).

* Statement
- *Donors* (P, As in Si): extra electron bound with a [[id:hydrogen-atom][hydrogenic]] energy \(E_d\approx13.6\frac{m^*}{m\epsilon_r^2}\) eV ≈ 45 meV: ionised at 300 K. *Acceptors* (B): bind a hole, \(E_a\approx45\) meV.
- *Neutrality*: \(p-n+N_D^+-N_A^-=0\). With \(np=n_i^2\) this is a quadratic; its positive root is the formula in the summary.
- *Fermi level*: \(E_F-E_i=k_BT\ln(n/n_i)\), where \(E_i\) is near mid-gap.

* Temperature regimes
| regime     | T range (Si, N_D = 10¹⁵) | n                    |
|------------+--------------------------+----------------------|
| freeze-out | below ≈ 100 K            | donors not ionised   |
| extrinsic  | ≈ 100–500 K              | n ≈ N_D (flat)       |
| intrinsic  | above ≈ 550 K            | n ≈ n_i, rising fast |
The extrinsic plateau is the useful operating range; its upper end limits Si devices to ~150–200 °C and motivates wide-gap SiC/GaN ([[id:wide-bandgap-power-devices][wide-bandgap devices]]).

* Worked example
Si, \(N_D=10^{16}\), \(N_A=4\times10^{15}\) cm\(^{-3}\) (compensated): \(n=6\times10^{15}\), \(p=(10^{10})^2/6\times10^{15}=1.7\times10^4\) cm\(^{-3}\). \(E_F-E_i=0.0259\ln(6\times10^5)=0.345\) eV; \(E_c-E_F=0.0259\ln(2.8\times10^{19}/6\times10^{15})=0.219\) eV.

* Engineering applications
Doping profiles define wells, sources/drains and channels in CMOS, emitters in solar cells, drift regions in power devices (low \(N_D\) for high breakdown), and the resistivity of substrates (\(\rho=1/q(n\mu_n+p\mu_p)\)).

* Pitfall
Compensation: adding acceptors to an n-type sample reduces \(n\) but *increases* impurity scattering, so mobility drops — net doping and total impurities are not the same.
