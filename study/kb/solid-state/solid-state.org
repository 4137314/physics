#+title: Semiconductor physics: the map of the subject
#+summary: Electrons in a periodic crystal form bands (Bloch); near the band edges they behave as free particles with effective mass; Fermi–Dirac statistics and doping fix the carrier densities; drift, diffusion and recombination move them; the pn junction is where all of this becomes a device.

* Overview
| block       | notes                                                         |
|-------------+---------------------------------------------------------------|
| band theory | Bloch theorem and bands, effective mass and holes             |
| carriers    | density of states and Fermi–Dirac, intrinsic and doped        |
| transport   | drift–diffusion and Einstein relation, generation and recomb. |
| junctions   | pn electrostatics, diode current, heterojunctions and wells   |
