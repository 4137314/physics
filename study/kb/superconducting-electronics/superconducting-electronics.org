#+title: Superconducting electronics: the map of the subject
#+summary: Superconductors carry current without resistance and quantise magnetic flux in units of \(\Phi_0=h/2e\); Josephson junctions switch in picoseconds dissipating \(\sim10^{-19}\) J, so logic that stores and moves single flux quanta (SFQ) runs at tens of GHz with orders of magnitude less energy per operation than CMOS — and it is the natural control electronics for superconducting qubits.

* Comparison with CMOS
| quantity         | CMOS (≈ 5 nm)         | RSFQ / ERSFQ             |
|------------------+-----------------------+--------------------------|
| switching energy | ≈ 10⁻¹⁶ J incl. wires | ≈ 2×10⁻¹⁹ J (I_c·Φ₀)     |
| clock            | 3–5 GHz               | 20–100 GHz demonstrated  |
| interconnect     | RC lines              | ballistic microstrip     |
| temperature      | 300 K                 | 4.2 K (cooling overhead) |
| density          | ≈ 10⁸ gates/mm²       | ≈ 10⁵–10⁶ JJ/cm²         |

* Engineering applications
Ultra-fast digital signal processing (RF digital receivers), cryogenic control and read-out of [[id:qubits-and-quantum-gates][qubits]], SQUID magnetometry (MEG brain imaging), voltage standards (Josephson arrays define the volt).
